WELCOME TO MAZEMAN

Get lost.
Have a good time.

Use arrow keys or WASD to move. On touchscreens, use the direction buttons below the maze.

  • Eat dots for 10 points and power pellets for 50.
  • Power pellets make ghosts flee for 6 seconds. Catch one for 200 points.
  • A ghost catches you? Lose a life and return to the start. You have 3 lives.
  • Clear every dot and pellet to reach the next level. The ghosts get faster.
  • Press P to pause or resume. After game over, press Enter to start fresh.
